Kingston, Jamaica

Jamaica is a vibrant island in the Caribbean Sea, famous for its stunning beaches, lush green mountains, tropical waterfalls, and warm sunshine. The island is rich in culture, with reggae music, delicious traditional dishes such as jerk chicken and ackee and saltfish, and a strong spirit of community. Jamaica is also home to world-famous athletes and musicians. Its capital city, Kingston, is known for its history, culture, and lively atmosphere.
