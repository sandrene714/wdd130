Our Mission

Our mission at Splash White Water Rafting is to offer memorable river experiences and encouraging teamwork, safety and love for nature.We believe every adventure should make memories that last a lifetime!

History

Splash White Water Rafting was born out of a passion for adventure and the outdoors. What was at first a small rafting outfit has become a destination for adventure lovers. Our aim is to bring people through exciting and memorable river experiences.
